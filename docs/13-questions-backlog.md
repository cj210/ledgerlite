# Questions Backlog

## Purpose

Capture open questions that require discussion or a deliberate decision during LedgerLite development.

This document prevents unresolved questions from being forgotten while allowing development to continue when an immediate decision is not required.

---

## Questions

**Status: Empty**

No open questions are currently recorded.

---

## Guidelines

- Add questions when an important decision is unresolved.
- Do not add questions that do not require a decision.
- Resolve questions when sufficient context is available.
- Record significant decisions in the decision log after resolution.
- Remove or mark resolved questions so the backlog reflects only open items.
